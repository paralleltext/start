Resources for language study
This website containes three programs I developed that proved useful to my own language studies.
The ParallelTextApp enables you to read through a text and a corresponding parallel translation. While reading you have access to a self chosen dictionary and, if applicable, a transliteration of the original.
As a final result after processing your complete text you can produce a HTML-file that combines the original text and its translation where all words in both texts are references to selected dictionaries. As an example of this whole procedure I give the text Selbstdenken by Arthur Schopenhauer. The original version I found here and its corresponding translation I found here.
There a numerous examples of books, novels, song texts and the like and there translated counterparts scattered over the internet. Having only a limited knowledge of the original language concerned, reading it accompanied by its translated counterpart can be worthwhile and encouraging.
The AddDictionaryApp changes all words in a plain text into references to a selected dictionary. As an example of this I again give the text Selbstdenken by Arthur Schopenhauer in this version.
This saves you the tedious job of looking up words in a dictionary.
Finally the RehearsalApp is a word rehearsal tool.
You can also use it to rehearse words using the Greek, Cyrillic, Hebrew or Arabic alphabets.
If you open this website in Microsoft Edge and click on this link you download a C# version of the above-mentioned apps.
It has largely the same functionality but the file handling is less cumbersome and prone to mistakes. The Ancient Greek alphabet is also somewhat more elaborated.
